# Examiner Critique & Revision Log

This document does two things the task requires:

1. Applies a **strict IBMYP Personal Project examiner rubric** to the *first draft* of the
   report, criticising each part against the published strand descriptors.
2. Records the **exact revisions** made in response — the changes that are already baked
   into [`01-personal-project-report.md`](./01-personal-project-report.md).

The rubric used is the current *MYP Projects guide* (first assessment 2021), max **8** per
criterion, judged on the "best‑fit" band descriptors (levels 7–8 = the top band).

---

## Part 1 — Strict critique of the first draft

I scored the first draft as a hostile examiner, defaulting to the *lower* band whenever a
strand was merely asserted rather than **evidenced** — because that is how real markbands
are applied.

### Criterion A — Planning (first‑draft score: **6/8**)

| Strand | Band descriptor (7–8) | First‑draft verdict | Deduction reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| A.i | States goal **and explains** how a personal interest led to it | Goal stated; interest was a single generic sentence ("I like the stock market") | Interest not *traced*; no personal narrative → drops to band 5–6 |
| A.ii | States product **and develops** appropriate success criteria | Product stated; criteria present but two (SC "good quality", "long enough") were **not measurable** | Non‑measurable criteria = criteria not truly "developed" |
| A.iii | **Detailed** plan for product *and its success criteria* | Timeline present but no milestones/gates, no risk handling | "Detailed" not met — a bare timeline is band 5 |

**Harshest note:** the draft *described* a plan and goals but did not prove they were
**rigorous** — no decision records, no measurable thresholds, no risk register. Band 7–8
rewards demonstrated rigour, not the existence of a plan.

### Criterion B — Applying skills (first‑draft score: **5/8**)

| Strand | Band descriptor (7–8) | First‑draft verdict | Deduction reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| B.i | **Explains how** ATL skills helped the *learning goal* | Listed "research skills" and "I did research" | Named a skill but did not **explain the mechanism** or show evidence → band 3–4 |
| B.ii | **Explains how** ATL skills helped the *product* | Mentioned "time management" generically | No artefact, no cause‑and‑effect linking skill → product outcome |

**Harshest note:** Criterion B is the most commonly under‑scored strand precisely because
students *list* skills. The draft did this. The command term is **explain how** — it
requires a traceable line from *skill applied* → *specific action* → *effect on goal/product*.

### Criterion C — Reflecting (first‑draft score: **6/8**)

| Strand | Band descriptor (7–8) | First‑draft verdict | Deduction reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| C.i | **Explains the impact** on self/learning | General "I learned a lot and enjoyed it" | Impact not specific or evidenced; no transfer beyond the project |
| C.ii | **Evaluates** product **against the success criteria** | Said "I met my goals" | This is a *claim*, not an *evaluation*; no per‑criterion judgement, no weaknesses |
| C.iii | States ways to **extend** | One line: "I could write more" | Thin; not a genuine extension of scope/skills |

**Harshest note:** C.ii was the weakest strand overall — the draft asserted success rather
than *evaluating against the eight criteria*, and named **no** limitations. An evaluation
with zero weaknesses reads as uncritical and is capped below the top band.

**First‑draft total: 17/24.** Solid, but not the 8/8/8 the brief demands.

## Part 2 — Revisions made (and where they now live)

Each critique above was addressed by a specific, verifiable change in the final report.

**Table — Critique → revision mapping**

| Strand | Problem in draft | Concrete fix in final report | Now at |
|---|---|---|---|
| A.i | Interest was generic | Added the traced personal narrative (the candlestick question; consumer→critic motivation) proving the interest *led to* the goal | §2 A.i |
| A.ii | Non‑measurable criteria | Rewrote **all 8 success criteria** with numeric thresholds *and* a verification method; added Appendix A matrix | §2 A.ii, Table 2 |
| A.iii | Bare timeline | Added milestones + **gate criteria** (Table 3) and a **risk register** with mitigations (Appendix C) | §2 A.iii, Figs 2, Table 3 |
| B.i | Skills listed, not explained | Rebuilt around **two** skills with a *mechanism* and evidence: source‑appraisal log (Table 4) and critical‑thinking comparison framework (Table 5, Fig 3) | §3 B.i |
| B.ii | Generic "time management" | Replaced with **cause→effect**: chapter template → clarity/risk SCs (Fig 4); weekly quota + burn‑down → recovered slippage (Fig 5) | §3 B.ii |
| C.i | "Learned a lot" | Three specific, evidenced impacts incl. **transfer** to another subject and the loss‑asymmetry insight (Table 6) | §4 C.i |
| C.ii | Claimed success | Full per‑criterion evaluation table **plus three named, evidenced weaknesses** | §4 C.ii, Table 7 |
| C.iii | "Write more" | Structured **extension roadmap** tied to the ATL skills each would demand (Table 8) | §4 C.iii |

**Additional quality upgrades in the rewrite**

- Added a **global‑context decision record** (Table 1) so the context choice is justified,
  not assumed.
- Added a **strand index** at the end of the report so an examiner can locate every strand
  against the rubric in seconds.
- Converted every diagram to **Mermaid** so the "diagrams" requirement renders natively and
  is reproducible, not a flat image.
- Wrote the tone to be **direct and professional** throughout, per the brief.
